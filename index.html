Pharmacy management system

Pharmacera

Inventory, sales and reporting in one Laravel application—built to handle the practical rules behind a pharmacy.

PHPLaravelMySQLEloquentFilamentTailwind CSS
How it's built

What it does

Pharmacera gives Admins, Pharmacists and Cashiers the tools to manage stock, sales and reporting with role-based access.

Built around

FIFO stock management, expiry tracking, barcode sales, audit logs, low-stock email alerts and Arabic RTL, French and English.

Engineering decisions

  • Batch-based inventory keeps stock quantities and expiry dates tied to individual medicine batches.
  • FIFO sales prioritize older stock when processing sales.
  • Role-based access separates what Admins, Pharmacists and Cashiers can do.

Community platform

DevDz

A place for Algerian developers to share projects, find jobs and ask useful questions.

LaravelBladeTailwind CSSJavaScript
How it's built

Features

Projects, jobs, questions, comments, voting, tags, city filtering and keyword search.

Making it work

Custom authentication with sessions, CSRF and middleware; Blade components styled with Tailwind CSS.

Engineering decisions

  • Authentication and protected actions are handled through sessions, CSRF protection and middleware.
  • Users can interact with content through comments and voting while access to protected actions is restricted to authenticated users.
  • Projects, jobs and questions are separated into distinct content types with keyword search, tags and city filtering.
